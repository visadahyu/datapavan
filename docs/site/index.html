Datapavan

See what the apps on your Android phone can reach and send, and stop it. Everything runs on the phone. No internet access, no account, no root.

What it checks

  • Every installed app: where it came from, its sensitive permissions, and the special access that lets an app read the screen or act for you.
  • Trackers and SDKs inside each app's code, matched on the phone.
  • VPN, proxy and circumvention apps, against their developers' official signing keys, so that a modified copy shows.
  • The phone itself: debugging, certificate authorities you installed, the security patch and the boot state.
  • How much data each app sent and received, and which apps you have not opened for months but still hold access.

What it helps you do

A short plan, the changes that remove the most risk first. Each one opens the right Android screen, where you decide. A journal on the phone keeps what you changed, so that you can undo it. The apps your phone needs are never touched.

What it never does

No analytics, no crash reports, no servers, no accounts. Nothing leaves the phone. Genuine Datapavan has no internet access and never asks for it. A copy that asks for it is not genuine.

Get it

  • GitHub Releases: the signed APK, its SHA-256 and the signed release notes.
  • F-Droid and IzzyOnDroid: coming with the first release.
  • Obtainium: add https://github.com/visadahyu/datapavan.

Check your copy

Every genuine copy is signed with one key. Its certificate's SHA-256 fingerprint is:

FF:DF:39:72:91:80:12:31:
AB:2D:81:20:8D:F8:AD:7E:
52:03:1D:3D:1E:D8:FD:62:
11:C3:29:FC:5A:14:CE:2D

Compare it with a tool other than Datapavan itself, since a modified copy can show any text: for example AppVerifier on the phone, or apksigner verify --print-certs on a computer. The same fingerprint is in the README and in every release's signed notes.

Security and privacy

Report a vulnerability privately to security@datapavan.org (OpenPGP key: openpgp-key.asc) or through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting. See the security policy and the privacy notes. This page sets no cookies and loads nothing from other servers.
