# Visadahyu Foundation: charter principles

> Status: **principles, not yet a legal entity.** This document is the public commitment that the
> foundation's statutes will be built on. Once the foundation is registered, these principles go into
> its statutes in a form that is hard to change.

## Why a foundation

Datapavan protects people from the commercial pressure to collect their data. The organisation behind
it must be immune to the same pressure. No investor should be able to demand an exit, and no single
paying party should be able to bend the product.

We admire Mozilla for what it achieved, and we learn from its main weakness. Most of Mozilla's income
has long come from search deals with Google, the company behind its main competitor. This charter is
written so that the same dependency cannot happen here.

## Structure

1. **The Visadahyu Foundation** will be a non-profit established in the European Union. It will own:
   - the source code copyrights it holds,
   - the *Datapavan* and *Visadahyu* trademarks and logos,
   - the **release signing keys**, which decide which updates are genuine,
   - governance of the project.
2. **A commercial company owned by the foundation** may sell enterprise editions, support and contracts. Its profits go to the foundation. It has no other shareholders who could demand a sale.
3. Until the foundation exists, the maintainer holds these assets and commits to transferring them to the foundation once it is registered.

## Revenue rules

1. **Diversification.** No single source may exceed 25 % of annual revenue.
   - *Transition:* this rule applies from the foundation's fourth full financial year, or earlier once annual revenue exceeds €250,000. Until then, a single source may be larger. That is unavoidable for a young project, and it is reported openly under rule 6.
   - *Definition:* a source is the **ultimate funder**. Grants passed on by an intermediary count towards the body that provides the money. For example, NLnet grants funded by the European Commission count as the European Commission.
2. **Forbidden revenue**, regardless of amount:
   - advertising, in any form;
   - selling, renting or sharing user data, including in "anonymised" or aggregated form;
   - payment for default placement of any service (search engines, browsers, assistants);
   - money from any party whose data collection Datapavan restricts, with one narrow exception: **certification fees** paid under the independence rules below.
3. **Grants are welcome,** within rule 1 once it applies. Public funding policies change.
4. **No investment that requires an exit** (sale or IPO). Only funding compatible with the foundation keeping control (steward ownership).
5. **Reserve fund:** each year part of the surplus goes into a reserve, until it covers two to three years of operation.
6. **Transparency:** every year the foundation publishes its total revenue by source category. Institutional sources above €1,000 a year are named, with amounts. **Individual donors are never named.** Only their total is published, because naming them could endanger people in restricted regions.

## Independence of certification

Device makers are among the parties whose data collection Datapavan restricts. Taking their money is
allowed only under all of these conditions:

- The fee schedule is fixed and published. The fee pays for a **review**, never for a label.
- The fee does not depend on the outcome. A failed review costs the same as a passed one.
- The criteria are public, and they apply equally to every applicant.
- Reviewers have no financial ties to the applicant.
- Every result is published, failures included, with the reasons.
- No certification fee counts as a donation, and none buys any influence on the product or the criteria.

## Governance

- Corporate members can never hold a majority of the board.
- Board members disclose conflicts of interest and abstain on affected decisions.
- Decisions with lasting effect on the project are recorded publicly (see [decisions/](decisions/)). They are made and approved by a named maintainer; drafting may be AI-assisted, which is disclosed.
- The project stays open source under Apache-2.0. The trademark, not the copyright, is the main tool for protecting users from imitations.

## Jurisdiction

The foundation will be established inside the EU. EU programmes on security and strategic autonomy may
limit participation to EU-established, EU-controlled entities (Horizon Europe, Article 22(5)). The
exact country and legal form will be chosen with legal advice.
