# Datapavan

> {{ $t.tagline }} {{ $t.lead }}

Datapavan is a free, open-source privacy app for Android phones, under the Apache License 2.0. It comes as editions of one app: Datapavan Basic, the released app, which never has internet access; Datapavan Advanced, not yet released, which adds a log of every app's connections, tracker blocking and encrypted DNS; and Datapavan Enterprise, for organisations, later. Everything runs on the phone, with no account and no root. Download it from this site or the repository's releases. A copy from anywhere is fine once its signing key matches the fingerprint the main page gives, and the app's "Is this copy genuine?" check tells.
