# Code of conduct

Datapavan is built for people of all nations, and so is its community.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and considerate, in disagreement too. Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Welcome newcomers, and people whose first language is not English.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Keep discussions on topic. Politics, religion and nationality are not grounds for exclusion. They are also not topics for project spaces, except where they directly affect users' safety.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or sexualised language and imagery.
- Publishing others' private information, including users' or contributors' locations and identities.
- Pressuring anyone in a restricted country to take risks.
- Deliberately sabotaging work or the project's security.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately through the form named in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), saying that the report is
about conduct, until a dedicated address exists. Reports are handled confidentially. Consequences range from a warning to a
permanent ban, depending on severity.

This code applies in all project spaces: the GitHub repository, chats, and events.
